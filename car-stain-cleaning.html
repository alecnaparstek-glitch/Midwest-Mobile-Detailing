Carpet and mats are part of the interior package

Carpet and floor mat cleaning with P&S Carpet Bomber is included in the Cabin Refresh and Full Reset. This helps address dirt that accumulates where shoes, bags and everyday use meet the cabin. If the vehicle has a concentrated spill or stubborn mark, mention it before booking rather than assuming a standard clean will resolve it.

When to choose the stain add-on

Heavy Stain Removal is an additional $40 with an interior or complete detail. Tell us what caused the stain if you know, how long it has been there and whether another cleaner has already been used. Photos help us understand the affected area and discuss realistic expectations before the appointment.

Cleaning cannot reverse every kind of damage

Some marks are permanent discoloration, bleaching or material wear. A cleaner cannot restore missing dye or repair damaged fibers. Old spills and unknown substances can also respond differently to cleaning. We assess the area and explain limitations rather than promise complete removal from a photo.

What to do before we arrive

Remove belongings and loose mats or covers that prevent access, and avoid adding more chemicals just before the visit. Let us know about damp areas, unusual odors or suspected mold so we can discuss whether the job is suitable. The listed packages do not promise mold remediation or biohazard cleanup. For ordinary interior dirt plus exterior grime, consider the Full Reset instead of booking two separate visits.

Questions about this service

Does an interior detail include heavy stain removal?

Regular carpet and mat cleaning is included. Heavy Stain Removal is a separate $40 add-on for interior and complete packages; results depend on the stain and material.
