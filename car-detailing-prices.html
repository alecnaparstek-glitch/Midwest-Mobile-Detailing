Starting prices cover the entry vehicle tier; the estimate changes with vehicle size, condition and extras. Use the services page to compare the inclusions before choosing an appointment.

What changes the final estimate

A large vehicle has more area to clean, and heavy soil can require more work than routine dirt. Tell us about stains, pet hair and unusually dirty areas before the visit. Heavy Stain Removal is $40 extra with interior or complete packages. Ceramic Wax is $20 extra with exterior or complete packages. Heavy pet hair removal is priced after inspection.

How to request a useful quote

Send your vehicle’s year, make and model, your service location and photos of the cabin and exterior. Include close-ups of any problem areas and say whether your priority is inside, outside or both. That gives us enough context to discuss the right service without asking you to guess a package from price alone.

Payment and ongoing upkeep

Payment is due after the detail, with no online payment required to request an appointment. The winter interior membership is $129 per month after a Cabin Refresh and includes one interior upkeep visit per month during winter. See the membership details for eligibility. Available appointment times are shown in the live booking calendar.
