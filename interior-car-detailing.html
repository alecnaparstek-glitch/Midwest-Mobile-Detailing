What the Cabin Refresh includes

The Midwest Cabin Refresh starts at $159 and focuses on the places you see and touch every day: seats, carpets, floor mats, door panels, dashboard and center console. Complete interior and crevice vacuuming removes loose debris before the surfaces receive more detailed attention. Interior glass, mirrors, vents and tight spaces are part of the package.

Choose the service around your vehicle’s condition

An interior detail is a good fit when crumbs, dust and tracked-in dirt have built up but you do not need an exterior wash. Carpets and floor mats receive cleaning with P&S Carpet Bomber. Stubborn stains and heavy pet hair can take additional work, so mention them when booking and send photos for a clearer estimate. Cleaning results depend on the material and the condition; permanent discoloration and wear may remain.
