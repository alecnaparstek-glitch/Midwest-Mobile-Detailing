Privacy Policy

Midwest Mobile Detailing · Johnson County, Kansas

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Midwest Mobile Detailing uses customer information to arrange and provide mobile detailing in Johnson County, Kansas. This policy explains how information is handled when you visit our website, book a service or contact us.

Information we collect

  • Booking details: your name, mobile phone number, service address, vehicle information, selected service and add-ons, appointment time and details needed to provide your service.
  • Communications: information you provide when you call or text about a booking or service.
  • Vehicle photos: photos of our detailing work, which may be used in marketing without displaying personal information.
  • Website and security information: our hosting and website providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser information and website usage. The booking system also uses an IP address to limit repeated booking attempts.

How we use information

We use your details to schedule and provide your detail, communicate about appointments, respond to questions, handle service concerns and protect the booking system from misuse. Appointment texts may include confirmations, arrival updates, delays, weather changes, cancellations and rescheduling.

Square and other service providers

Our booking system sends your name, phone number, vehicle and service address, selected services and appointment information to Square to create and manage your appointment. No card details are collected by this website’s appointment form. Payment is due after service.

Square handles information under its own Privacy Notice for customers. Square may retain booking, customer or transaction records under its own policies, even after we delete records we manage directly.

Cloudflare hosts and protects the website and provides website analytics. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google to retrieve those fonts. These providers may process technical information needed to deliver their services. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Privacy Policy.

We do not sell customer information or share it for other businesses’ marketing. We use service providers as described above to operate the website and manage bookings.

Vehicle photos used in marketing

We use photos of vehicles to show our detailing work. We do not display personal information in marketing photos. If you have a question or concern about a photo of your vehicle, call or text us.

How long we keep information

We delete customer records we manage directly soon after service, unless you are a recurring customer. For recurring customers, we keep the details needed to arrange ongoing service. This practice does not set the retention periods for Square or other providers, which handle their own records under their policies.

Our website infrastructure also holds temporary booking records to prevent duplicate appointments, recover interrupted submissions and limit misuse.

Your questions and requests

To ask about your information, correct your details, request deletion or discuss a vehicle photo, call or text Midwest Mobile Detailing at (913) 905-9935. We handle requests individually and may need to verify that the request relates to your records. If information is held by Square or another provider, we can explain which provider is involved; its own process and retention rules may apply.

Policy updates

We may update this page when our practices change. The date above shows when this policy was last updated. For service and booking policies, see our Terms of Service.
