How to Prepare for a Mobile Car Detail

A few things ready. A smoother detail.

Home / Detailing Guides

Prepare for your Midwest Mobile Detailing appointment: water and power access, vehicle belongings, parking, weather and booking details.

Have water and electricity available

We bring the detailing equipment and products. You provide access to an outdoor water hookup and a standard electrical outlet at the service location. If either is unavailable, tell us before the appointment so we can discuss the location rather than discover the issue on arrival.

Clear the cabin before the visit

Remove cash, valuables, documents and loose belongings from seats, door pockets and the cargo area. Please remove child seats yourself before the appointment. There is no need to clean the car first; clearing belongings gives us access to the surfaces included in your package. Point out fragile items, damaged trim or any area you want us to avoid.

Choose a suitable place to work

Park where there is room to access the vehicle and work around it. For apartments, offices and other shared properties, confirm that detailing and water use are permitted. Secure pets away from the work area and let us know about access instructions, gates or parking restrictions ahead of time.

Share concerns and check your booking result

Send photos of heavy soil, stains or pet hair and tell us about any prior cleaning attempts. Check the booking result: Appointment Booked means confirmed; Appointment Requested means awaiting approval. Keep your mobile number available for updates. Weather or an earlier appointment can affect timing, and we will text if a change is needed. Call or text (913) 905-9935 as soon as possible to cancel or reschedule. Payment is due after completion.

Questions about this service

Do I have to supply cleaning products?

No. We bring our equipment and cleaning products. Please have an outdoor water hookup and standard electrical outlet available.
