Midwest Mobile Detailing · Johnson County, Kansas

These terms describe detailing services provided by Midwest Mobile Detailing in Johnson County, Kansas. Please read them before booking. Questions? Call or text (913) 905-9935.

Booking and appointment updates

Online appointments are arranged through Square. Check the result after submitting: “Appointment Booked” means your appointment is confirmed; “Appointment Requested” means it is awaiting approval. Provide accurate contact details, vehicle information and a service address where we can reach you.

Your arrival window is the first 30 minutes of your selected appointment time. We use your mobile number for appointment updates, including delays, weather changes, cancellations and rescheduling. If weather or an unexpected issue affects your appointment, we will contact you to arrange the next steps.

Prices and payment

Website prices and booking estimates depend on your selected package, vehicle size and add-ons. Heavy pet hair removal is priced after inspection. Any additional condition-based charges require your approval before the extra work is performed.

Payment is due after the detail is complete. No online payment is required when requesting an appointment. Monthly maintenance is available by arrangement after your first detail.

Cancellations and rescheduling

We do not charge cancellation fees. To cancel or move an appointment, call or text (913) 905-9935 as soon as possible. If we do not answer, please leave a message.

Service location and vehicle access

Please provide access to the vehicle, an outdoor water hookup and a standard electrical outlet at the service location. Let us know before your appointment if you cannot provide these. Please remove personal belongings and valuables and tell us about existing damage or areas that need special care.

Service results and missed spots

Results depend on your vehicle’s condition and the service selected. Some stains, wear or existing damage may remain after cleaning. Detailing does not repair existing damage or guarantee removal of every stain or scratch.

If we miss a spot, contact us within 24 hours after your service is completed. We will arrange a reclean of the missed areas at no additional charge. Call or text the number above so we can review the issue and arrange a time.

Photos and customer information

We use photos of vehicles to show our work in marketing without displaying personal information. Our Privacy Policy explains how we use booking details and photos and how to contact us about your information.

Questions about your service
